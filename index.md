# PeaBox AI Workbench

Open-source AI workspace for Ollama, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, LiteLLM, Open WebUI, OpenRouter and self-hosted LLMs.

PeaBox gives you one focused workspace for conversations with local, remote and
cloud models — your models, your APIs, one workbench.

# Pricing

PeaBox AI Workbench is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.

- No license fee
- No subscription
- No telemetry, accounts or phone-home requirement
- No paid tier or feature gating

Your only costs are the ones you choose:

- **Local models via Ollama** — free aside from your own hardware and electricity.
- **Cloud providers** — you pay the provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, OpenRouter, etc.) directly for API usage. PeaBox never sees your API keys beyond storing them locally in your operating system's credential store.
